# Customer Complaint Handling Policy

## Purpose
This policy describes how customer complaints are received, triaged, and
resolved, and the service levels customers can expect.

## Intake Channels
Complaints may arrive via the support email inbox, in-app support chat, phone
line, or escalation from a sales or success manager. All channels feed into
the same ticketing system so nothing is handled in isolation.

## Triage and Severity
Complaints are triaged into three severity levels:
- **Critical** — service outage, data loss, security concern, or safety issue.
  Acknowledged within 1 hour, updates every 2 hours until resolved.
- **High** — feature broken, billing error, repeated failures blocking the
  customer's work. Acknowledged within 4 business hours.
- **Standard** — general dissatisfaction, feature requests framed as
  complaints, minor bugs. Acknowledged within 1 business day.

## Response Process
1. Acknowledge receipt and confirm understanding of the issue back to the
   customer in their own words.
2. Investigate using logs, account history, and if needed, engineering
   escalation.
3. Communicate a resolution or a clear next step with a timeframe — never
   leave a complaint without an update for more than the SLA window.
4. Confirm resolution with the customer before closing the ticket.

## Escalation
A complaint should be escalated to a manager if:
- The customer explicitly requests escalation.
- The same issue has recurred three or more times for that customer.
- Resolution requires a policy exception (refund above standard threshold,
  contract term change).

## Documentation
Every complaint is logged with root cause, resolution, and time-to-resolution
metrics. Recurring root causes are reviewed monthly with the product team to
identify systemic fixes rather than repeatedly patching symptoms.

## Tone and Conduct
Support staff should acknowledge frustration without being defensive, avoid
placing blame on the customer, and never promise a fix timeline they cannot
confirm with engineering.
