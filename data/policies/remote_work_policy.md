# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose
This policy sets expectations for employees working remotely, including
availability, equipment, and expense reimbursement.

## Eligibility
Remote work arrangements are approved by an employee's manager based on role
requirements. Fully remote, hybrid, and occasional work-from-home arrangements
are all covered under this policy.

## Availability and Communication
- Remote employees are expected to be reachable during their team's agreed
  core hours (typically a 4-hour overlap window across time zones).
- Status should be kept current in the team chat tool (available, in a
  meeting, away).
- Video should be on for team meetings where feasible; audio-only is
  acceptable for large all-hands calls.

## Equipment and Expenses
- The company provides a laptop and standard peripherals for remote employees.
- A one-time home office stipend is available for desk, chair, and monitor;
  submit receipts through the expense system within 60 days of purchase.
- Internet and phone costs are not reimbursed unless the role specifically
  requires dedicated infrastructure (e.g., on-call engineers).

## Security Requirements
Remote work must comply with the Information Security Policy, including
MFA, disk encryption, and never accessing Confidential data over unsecured
public Wi-Fi without a company VPN connection.

## Performance Expectations
Remote work is evaluated the same way as in-office work: by outcomes and
deliverables, not hours logged or visible activity. Managers and employees
should agree on clear goals during 1:1s to keep expectations aligned.

## Travel to Company Offices
Remote employees are expected to travel to a company office for quarterly
team gatherings where applicable; travel and lodging costs for these events
are covered by the company per the standard travel policy.
